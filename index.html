Rescue Driver

Rescue Driver is a scenic driving game where skill, focus and calm decision-making matter. A group of people is trapped on a mountain top due to heavy snowfall, jungle environments and vehicle breakdown. You play as a driver on a rescue journey through difficult terrain to reach them and bring them safely home.
